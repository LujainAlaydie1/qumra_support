Why can't I see my photos immediately?

Qumra is a disposable camera experience. Your photos "develop" throughout the day and are only revealed between 12:00 AM and 01:00 AM.

What happens to my photos after 1:00 AM?

To preserve the ephemeral nature of film, the previous day's photos are destroyed at 01:00 AM. Ensure you save your favorites to your iOS Photo Library before then!

Does the app require an internet connection?

No. Qumra is designed to work fully offline. All processing and storage happen on your device.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 27, 2026

1. Zero Data Collection

We believe your memories are yours alone. Qumra does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, location information, or photos to external servers. Everything stays on your device.

2. Permissions

The app requires Camera access to take photos and Photo Library access to save your developed films. These permissions are used exclusively for app functionality.

3. Local Storage

Photos are stored locally using SwiftData. We have no access to these files. When you delete the app or when the "Golden Hour" ends, data is handled by the iOS system storage rules.

4. No Tracking

There are no third-party analytics, ads, or tracking pixels in Qumra. We don't want your data; we want you to enjoy the moment.
